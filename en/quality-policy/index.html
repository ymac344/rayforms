Quality Policy Statement

R.A.Y. Advanced Formwork Industries Ltd. produces portable precast structures and elements, and is also a contractor for construction works.

The company regards achieving a high level of quality in its projects as the principal way to earn the trust and satisfaction of its clients. The company believes this is an essential condition for its continued prosperity and development.

The company sees as a strategic goal meeting its clients' requirements and expectations with a high standard of finish, continuous and dedicated attention throughout the stages of the work, meeting the schedule for handing over the site, and providing good service to the client after the project is delivered.

The company's management believes that involving employees in establishing and operating the quality system is an essential condition for its success.

The company's management believes in the continual improvement and development of the company's systems in general and of its employees in particular.

All the procedures in the quality manual were formulated by the company's management, and the company's aim is that these procedures be strictly followed.

The purpose of the procedures in the company's quality manual is to establish a quality-assurance system that maintains a level of quality meeting the requirements of Israeli Standard ISO 9001:2008, and to set out the methods and procedures through which the company's quality objectives will be achieved.

All company employees are committed to implementing the requirements set out in the quality manual, and to the quality assurance specified at every stage of the work that relates to, or is affected by, their areas of activity.

Resources will be allocated for quality assurance as needed, in order to put the requirements of the standard into practice.

The company's management will make sure all employees are informed of the details of the quality-assurance procedure through ongoing training and refresher activities.

The organization's management is committed to a process of continual improvement of the effectiveness of the management system, using tools defined in the company's quality manual, such as management reviews and internal quality audits, and resources allocated for this purpose.

The organization's management is committed to meeting all the legal requirements of the State of Israel that relate to the organization's field of work.

The company's management will make sure the procedures are known and understood by all employees in their relevant areas of work.

The company's management will monitor the implementation of the quality policy in its regular meetings. In addition, quality assurance will be reviewed periodically in management review meetings.

Signed,
Eng. Boris WeinbergChief Executive Officer
