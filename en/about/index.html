About R.A.Y. (Advanced Formwork Industries) Ltd.

R.A.Y. (Advanced Formwork Industries) Ltd. produces portable precast structures and elements, and is also a contracting company for construction works.

  • The company holds contractor classification Branch 100, G-5, and is a recognized contractor for government works in that classification.
  • The company holds the ISO 9001 quality standard from the Standards Institution of Israel.
  • The plant is certified to Israeli Standard 1923 Part 2.
  • Our company is a manufacturer approved by the Israel Electric Corporation to produce precast transformer rooms.
  • Our company is a manufacturer approved by the Home Front Command to produce institutional and residential protected spaces.

Areas of experience

The company has extensive experience in construction and industrialized building:

  • Design and manufacture of formwork for concrete works, and development of advanced working methods.
  • Manufacture of precast elements.
  • Manufacture and supply of portable concrete buildings.
  • Contracting works in building and infrastructure – design, technology and on-site execution.
  • The company carries out projects using the design-build method.
  • The company carries out projects that include the design and installation of building systems such as plumbing and fire protection, elevators, electrical systems and air-conditioning systems of all types (VRF).

Personnel and resources

Personnel

The company is made up of a multidisciplinary team of engineers, architects, site managers and professional crews who travel throughout the country and are responsible for assembly works, conventional construction, site development and finishes.

The company's plant

The company's plant for producing portable buildings and precast elements is located in the Har Yona industrial zone in Nazareth Illit (now Nof HaGalil), where a large part of the company's activity is concentrated: management offices, design, sales and production.
An additional plant, covering more than 5,000 m², is located in the Mevo'ot HaGilboa industrial zone.

The company's advantages

Industrialized methods

The products go through quality-control processes that ensure a high standard of product.

Execution time

Our methods make it possible to complete many tasks on minimal schedules, save labor and maintain the highest quality of work.

Using our methods makes it possible to work with a small crew, which allows projects to be carried out at various sites with as little disruption as possible to day-to-day operations.

The buildings can serve any function required by the client's program: classrooms, offices, meeting rooms, living quarters and more.

Modular buildings

In recent years, a large part of the company's activity has been the production of modular (portable) buildings. The company has gained extensive experience in this field and has a strong reputation in the industry.
